What happens in the session

A situational read, not a skills test.

We begin by assessing how you're currently using AI, what's working, what's creating friction, and what's being avoided entirely. This isn't about grading your prompt craft. It's about mapping how AI is actually showing up in your working week.

You Leave With
—A clear picture of your current AI usage and blind spots.
—The business outcomes you're actually optimizing for.
—The real constraints shaping decisions right now.

Where AI fits, and where it doesn't.

With a clear picture of your current state, we map your business priorities and constraints against what AI can actually do, not in theory, but in your specific operating context. No generic frameworks. No hype-driven use cases.

You Leave With
—A map of where AI fits your actual business model.
—Where AI introduces risk you haven't accounted for.
—A reusable framework for evaluating AI fit going forward.

Definition, not ambiguity.

The session ends with definition. You'll know what to change, what to ignore, and what to test, with a prioritised next move that fits your real operating conditions. No ten-step playbook. One decision, made cleanly.
