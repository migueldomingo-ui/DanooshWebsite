AI Strategic Diagnostic

Paid clarity for
serious leaders.

A focused, structured working session designed to bring clarity to complex decisions around AI, strategy, and leverage. This is where most serious work begins.

You bring one real decision that's been stuck, something with real stakes and real constraints. We work through it together, using AI as a thinking partner. The goal isn't to teach you AI. It's to use AI to help you think more clearly about the specific problem in front of you.

You leave with a mental model, a prioritized next move, and the clarity to stop second-guessing whether you're "doing AI right." This isn't training. It's enablement.

90-minute live, 1:1 working session. No prep required. You bring one real decision. We work through it together using AI as a thinking partner. You leave with a clear next move, a reusable mental model, and the confidence to apply it to the next decision on your own.

Founders, owner-operators, and senior executives who have tried the tools and aren't seeing the lift they expected. Leaders who have a real decision that's been stuck and want to work through it with someone who understands both AI and how real businesses operate under pressure.

The AI Team Sprint

Focused work with
leadership teams.

Workshops, strategy engagements, and embedded support for leadership teams, focused on judgment, governance, and how AI actually gets adopted.

This isn't a training program or a keynote. It's structured working time with your team, designed around the specific decisions and challenges you're actually facing. Carefully scoped.

Scoped individually based on team size, context, and objectives. Typically includes a pre-engagement diagnostic, one or more working sessions with the leadership team, and a structured output (a shared mental model, a prioritized decision framework, or a governance approach) that the team can use going forward.

P&L owners whose teams are moving in different directions on AI and need a shared orientation. Organizations that have made or are about to make significant AI investments and want to ensure their leadership team has the judgment to guide those investments well. Most engagements come through existing clients or trusted introductions.

Team engagements are scoped individually. The best starting point is a conversation. Reach out directly and describe the challenge, team size, what's at stake, and what you're hoping to achieve. If it's a good fit, we'll scope something together.
